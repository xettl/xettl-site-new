A financial life that moves with you.

The world is your oyster. We're building Xettl to help you explore it with greater financial confidence.

Our vision

One banking relationship.
Wherever life takes you.

Even when borders grow more complicated, the world keeps becoming more connected. People build lives, support loved ones, and spend across countries and currencies.

Pandemics, wars, and changing immigration rules have not ended the dream of moving abroad. Our vision is a banking relationship you can trust throughout that journey, not just for one move, but for life.

Our mission

Help you build strong credit, make more of your money, and never feel financially stranded in a new country.

Stronger credit
Help you work toward your best credit health through habits and decisions that support your goals.
More from your money
Help you understand competitive saving and investment opportunities, including their costs and risks.
Confidence across borders
Guidance designed to keep you from feeling financially locked out, stranded, or confused in an unfamiliar system.

Personal financial guidance shouldn't be a privilege.

Private banking has traditionally served the wealthiest. We believe AI agents can make personalized financial guidance accessible to everyone.

Our ambition is to earn your trust with more than your money: to help you understand your options and make informed decisions, wherever life takes you.

Starting in the U.S.
Built for a lifetime.

We're beginning in the United States, a country shaped by generations of immigrants. We're building for students, workers, partners and families making a longer-term move—not only for one kind of newcomer.

Our ambition reaches further: to be with you from the moment you plan a move, through the life you build, for as long as you choose us.
