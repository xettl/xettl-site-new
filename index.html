A few things to know.

Who are you building Xettl for?

People moving to the U.S. for a longer stay: students, workers, partners and families. Whether you’re planning your move or already here, we’d love to hear what you need. Tourist visits aren’t our focus yet; final account eligibility has not been determined.

When can I start using Xettl?

Join the waitlist and choose product updates if you’d like to hear when applications open. We’ll share eligibility, pricing and availability before you apply.

Would I need to be in the U.S. to apply?

Our goal is to support applications before you arrive as well as after. Required documents, supported countries, timing and eligibility would depend on the final program and banking partner. A visa alone would not guarantee approval.

What if I don’t have a U.S. phone number?

We plan to offer an optional eSIM in the app, or let you use an existing number. Device compatibility, coverage, pricing and availability are still being worked out.

What about credit guidance and rent reporting?

Both are planned for a later phase and are not available now. We’ll share details when the scope and timing are confirmed. No credit score, credit decision or reporting outcome is guaranteed.

Is Xettl a bank?

No. Xettl is not a bank and does not currently offer financial services. Future banking services would require a regulated financial-institution partner, with terms, fees and eligibility disclosed before you apply.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You’ll record your interest in Xettl and share what you wish banking did better. Your name, email and written response stay private. Newsletter and launch/product emails are separate, optional choices, and you can unsubscribe at any time. There’s no announced launch date yet.
